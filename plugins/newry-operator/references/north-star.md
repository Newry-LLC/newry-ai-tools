## North Star Statement

**The AI-native boutique. Exceptional for clients. Exceptionally profitable for us.**

---

## Business Outcome Target

**2x profit per consultant by May 2027 vs. FY2025 baseline.**

This is the number the program is ultimately accountable to. Everything else — adoption, quality scores, tool coverage — is a leading indicator toward it. Baseline to be established against FY2025 actuals.

---

## Leading Indicators

Two proxies that tell you you're on track before the business outcomes are visible:

**1. Active weekly usage rate**
% of Newry consultants with ≥1 tool run in the trailing 7 days (30-day rolling average).
Measures adoption — are people actually using the tools?

**2. Eval pass rate**
% of skill runs that pass quality review against defined eval criteria.
Measures quality — is the work actually better, or just faster?

## Why "same team, materially better work, the P&L shows it"

Three things packed into one sentence:

**"Same team"** — this is not a headcount story. The program doesn't require hiring; it makes the people already here more capable. That's the pitch to leadership and the constraint that makes the economics work.

**"Materially better work"** — better is the point, not just faster. Speed that doesn't produce better client outcomes doesn't justify the investment. Quality is non-negotiable (Principle 3).

**"The P&L shows it"** — the program is accountable to business outcomes, not just usage metrics. If adoption is high but margins don't move, something is wrong — either the tools aren't changing how time is spent, or the freed capacity isn't being redirected. Either way, it's a program failure, not a success.
